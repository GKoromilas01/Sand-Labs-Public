VIRTUAL MONEY / NO WAGERS PLACED

Paper strategy lab

Strategy research plan

The goal is to test prediction quality, available prices and staking together. Profitability differs from hit rate. Our current estimates do not establish a betting edge.

  • Start with flat virtual stakes; record net return, ROI, costs and losses.
  • Keep odds and observation times before kickoff. A snapshot is not a guaranteed price or a verified closing line.
  • For calibrated probability p and decimal odds d, expected net return per euro is p × d −1. Uncalibrated probabilities do not justify Kelly staking.
  • Future tests may compare observed price movement and capped fractional-Kelly simulations on unseen matches. These strategy comparisons are not implemented yet.

More data can help, but does not guarantee accuracy or profit.

Finding and assessing more sources

ESPN currently supplies fixture/statistical data; that role is not graded as a published tipster. Additional public prediction sites can be considered after checking access, terms, timestamps and whether their picks match our fixtures and markets.

External published picks are captured research snapshots. Opening the app refreshes match results used to score them; it does not automatically scrape BBC or other prediction articles. More sources will be added only with verifiable pre-match records.

Future comparisons should use overlapping fixtures and forward periods. A high hit rate can reflect short-priced favourites and does not establish betting profit.

YOUR RECORD / YOUR DEVICE

Data & help

Where is the database?

The app combines a shared published starting record with a database saved in this browser using IndexedDB. Source results and new forecasts fetched here are saved on this device. Other browsers do not automatically receive them.

The private GitHub SQLite database is a separate analysis mirror. Export this device record to transfer it for review and database reconciliation. No repository token or account password is stored in the app.

When does data refresh?

Only when you open the app or press Refresh. There are no scheduled jobs, timed polling or background collection. Pending fixtures from the last seven days are checked; older unresolved fixtures need manual review.

Up to10 upcoming fixtures per Athens day are selected, with the five major leagues first, then UEFA competitions, at least30minutes before kickoff. This is a fixed coverage rule, not an easy-match or betting-value strategy.

What is checked?

ESPN fixture status, available standings, recent scores and available price snapshots. Final scores are accepted only when marked complete. Extra-time, penalty or changed-schedule cases are held for review. Narrative news, suspensions and lineups are not automatically verified.
