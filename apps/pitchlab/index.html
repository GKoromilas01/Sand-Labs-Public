How this version works

Choose matches in the five major European leagues. Record probabilities before kickoff, then enter final scores in chronological order. Results update team ratings; saved predictions stay unchanged for honest evaluation.

No live fixtures, automatic news collection or daily background updates are connected. Teams start equally rated until you enter historical results. The draw formula and home advantage are initial assumptions, not trained or calibrated estimates. More data does not guarantee improvement.

Use verified historical results to warm up ratings first. All data stays in this browser; no betting, account or AI service is connected.

Log loss and Brier score: lower is better. Compare against the uniform three-outcome baseline, then evaluate over many unseen matches. Correct-result percentage alone is insufficient.
